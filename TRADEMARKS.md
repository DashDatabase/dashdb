# Trademark policy

DashDB's code is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The DashDB name and logo are not.
The license lets you use, change and share the code; this policy covers the brand, so that people
can tell the official DashDB apart from changed versions of it. As section 7 of the AGPL allows,
the license grants no rights to these trademarks.

This is the plain-language version. It is not legal advice, and when in doubt, ask (see the end).

## What this covers

- The name **DashDB**
- The DashDB logo and mark (the files in [`packages/ui/src/brand`](packages/ui/src/brand) and the
  app icons generated from them)
- The **dashdb.dev** domain

## Fine without asking

- **Saying what is true.** "Works with DashDB", "built on DashDB", "a fork of DashDB", "exported
  from DashDB", and the name in articles, talks, tutorials, reviews and comparisons.
- **Linking to DashDB** with the name or logo, as long as it doesn't suggest we endorse you.
- **Running DashDB unchanged**, including the official Docker image, for yourself, your team or
  your company, and sharing unchanged copies with the name and logo intact.
- **Changing it for your own team.** A modified version you run internally can keep the name.
- **Contributing.** Pull requests to this repository don't need any permission.

## Needs permission

- **Offering a modified version to other people under the DashDB name**, as a hosted service or a
  download. Rename it and replace the logo first (see below).
- **Using DashDB, or something confusingly similar, in your own product, company, domain or
  package name**, like "DashDB Pro" or "dashdb-cloud.com".
- **Using the logo in a way that suggests we made, sponsor or endorse your product.**
- **Selling merchandise** with the name or logo.

## If you fork

You are welcome to fork DashDB. If you offer your changed version to other people:

1. Give it a different name and replace the logo and app icons.
2. Say it is based on DashDB, which is encouraged ("Based on DashDB"), but not that it is DashDB.
3. Keep the AGPL's obligations: your users must be able to get your source code.

## Other trademarks

PostgreSQL, MySQL, Prisma, Drizzle, DBML and other product names are trademarks of their owners.
DashDB uses them only to describe compatibility.

## Questions

Email **licensing@dashdb.dev**. If you're unsure whether something is fine, ask: the answer is
usually yes.
