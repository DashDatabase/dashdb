## What the agreement will say, in plain language

- **You keep the copyright** to your contribution. You can use your own code however you like.
- **You give DashDB a license to it**: permission to use, change and distribute your contribution,
  including under licenses other than the AGPL. DashDB Community stays AGPL-3.0; this permission
  is what lets the same code also ship in DashDB's paid cloud, Team and Enterprise editions, which
  are developed separately under a commercial license.
- **You confirm you're allowed to contribute it**: you wrote it, or you have the right to submit
  it (for example, your employer agrees), and you've told us about any third-party code or
  license that applies.
- **Patents**: you license any patents of yours that the contribution needs, so people can use it.
- **No obligations otherwise**: you don't have to provide support, and the contribution is given
  "as is".

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA bot comments on it; reply with the sentence it gives you:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

You only sign once. The bot records your GitHub username and the date, and the check passes on
this and every later pull request.
