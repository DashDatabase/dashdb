DashDB is a visual database schema editor. You draw tables and relationships on a canvas (or
edit the same schema as code), save a **baseline** of what is in production, and DashDB writes
the migration from that baseline to your design. Before you ship it, every migration is checked
against <RuleCount /> safety rules that know how Postgres and MySQL take locks.

The editor runs entirely in your browser. There is no signup, and your schemas are saved on
your device, never on a server.

## What DashDB is good at

- **Designing a new schema** with primary keys, foreign keys, indexes, unique and check
  constraints, enums and comments, then exporting it as SQL, Prisma, Drizzle or DBML.
- **Changing an existing schema safely.** Import what you have, edit it, and get a migration
  with its risks spelled out: table rewrites, long locks, and steps that lose data.
- **Explaining a schema** to your team, with a diagram you can export as PNG or SVG, or paste
  into a pull request as a Mermaid ER diagram.

## Supported databases

Projects target **PostgreSQL** or **MySQL 8**. You can import from either dialect's DDL, from
Prisma and DBML files, or straight from a running database, and convert between them with the
[free converter tools](/tools).
